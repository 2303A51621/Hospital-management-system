Introduction / Background

Contemporary healthcare facilities grapple with fragmented clinical workflows, siloed patient records, delayed emergency dispatch, and error-prone paper prescriptions.

This capstone engineers an enterprise-grade, multi-role hospital management platform synchronized with a grounded Clinical AI Assistant powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Gemini LLM. It guarantees strict role separation and auditable data boundaries.

Problem Statement

  • • Disconnected Clinical Silos: Outpatient, inpatient, lab, pharmacy, and billing operate on disparate, non-communicating tools.
  • • Medication Safety Vulnerabilities: Absence of electronic 5-rights verification leads to adverse clinical drug errors.
  • • Hallucination Risks in Medical AI: Generic chatbots invent drug dosages without grounded medical knowledge retrieval.
  • • Authorization Gaps: Lack of cryptographic RBAC and immutable audit logs causes data leakage and compliance failure.
